FORGE

Privacy Policy · Last updated 24 September 2026

FORGE is a project planning app made by Umar Abdulazeez (House of Prime). This page explains what the app stores about you and why. The short version: only what it needs to work, never sold, never used for advertising.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address, the name you choose, and an encrypted password (handled by our sign-in provider, so we never see the password itself).
  • What you create: your builds, areas, tasks and templates, so they're there when you open the app again.
  • Your subscription status: whether you have FORGE Pro. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card details.

Who processes it

  • Supabase — hosts the database and sign-in.
  • RevenueCat — keeps track of subscriptions for us, using an anonymous account ID.
  • Apple — processes payments through your Apple ID.

None of them may use your data for their own purposes. We don't use advertising or tracking SDKs, and we don't sell or share your data.

What we don't collect

No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or advertising identifiers. When you share a blueprint, the image is created on your phone and goes only where you send it.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → your name → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything in it straight away. A Pro subscription must be cancelled separately in your Apple ID settings.

Children

FORGE isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their data.
